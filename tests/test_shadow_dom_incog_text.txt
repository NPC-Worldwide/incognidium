Shadow DOM Pseudo-elements Demo
::part Pseudo-element
The pseudo-element allows styling of shadow DOM parts from the ::part
outside:
• - Styles the "button" part my-element::part(button)
• - Styles the "header" part my-element::part(header)
::slotted Pseudo-element
The pseudo-element styles content slotted into shadow DOM: ::slotted
• - Styles elements with class "highlight" ::slotted(.highlight)
• - Styles all slotted elements ::slotted(*)
Note: These pseudo-elements are part of the CSS Shadow Parts and Scoping
specifications. They enable encapsulation while allowing selective styling from
outside the shadow root.
Shadow DOM pseudo-elements bridge the gap between encapsulation and customization,
allowing component authors to expose specific styling hooks while keeping implementation
details private.
