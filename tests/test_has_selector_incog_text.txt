:has() Relational Pseudo-class Demo
What is :has()?
The pseudo-class selects elements that contain descendants matching :has()
the inner selector:
• - Selects sections containing images section:has(img)
• - Selects labels containing inputs label:has(input)
• - Selects divs with .highlight children div:has(.highlight)
